Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Kampala and surrounding areas in Uganda, including Gayaza, Wakiso, Mukono, and other nearby locations. Contact us to confirm if we cover your area.

How do I request a quote?

You can request a free quote by contacting us via WhatsApp, phone, or email. We'll discuss your project requirements and provide a detailed estimate within 24-48 hours.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes! We offer 24/7 emergency plumbing and electrical services. Contact our emergency hotline for immediate assistance with urgent issues.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept mobile money (MTN, Airtel), bank transfers, and cash payments. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger projects.
